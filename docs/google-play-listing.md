Koschei the Deathless shattered the Alatyr stone — and the roads between the worlds tangled. Spirits and gods of seven mythologies escaped and scattered across the whole planet. Become a Catcher of the Order and bring them back!

DUKHOLOV is a spirit-catching adventure on a map of the entire world. No GPS and no walking outside: open the World Atlas, choose a continent and a famous city — Moscow, Athens, Reykjavik, Dublin, Cairo, Beijing, Mexico City and more — and step through the Gates.

EXPLORE THE WHOLE WORLD
• World Atlas: start anywhere and teleport to any place on Earth later
• Walk and run with a mini joystick on the left or right (WASD and arrows on PC)
• Every step counts: hatch cocoons and climb the Season Path as you travel
• Wellsprings, shrines and Rifts stand at real landmarks around the globe

CATCH SPIRITS OF 7 MYTHOLOGIES
• 441 spirits and gods — Slavic, Greek, Norse, Celtic, Egyptian, Chinese and Aztec
• Meet any of them anywhere: a Greek nymph in Tokyo, an Aztec spirit in Paris
• Six elements: Fire, Water, Forest, Wind, Current and Shadow
• Throw a charm into the shrinking ring — or catch spirits in AR with your camera
• Rare, shining and Nav-shadowed forms; weather and time of day change who appears

GROW YOUR COLLECTION
• Level up and evolve your spirits, equip amulets, learn a second special move
• A buddy spirit travels with you and finds gifts
• Bestiary, photo album and the Chronicle — a story campaign with mentor Velimir

RIFTS AND BATTLES
• Rifts — powerful bosses to defeat alone or with up to 4 Catchers
• 3-on-3 shrine battles and Nav invasions

THE ALATYR STONE
• Shards from all Catchers go into one shared stone: every facet opens the road to a mythology
• Each Alatyr season brings a new mythology — the next one is still hidden behind “?”
• The season finale is a battle with Koschei himself

CLANS AND LEAGUE
• Join a mythology clan: Perun’s Druzhina, Phalanx of Olympus, Hird of Asgard, Brotherhood of the Oak, Guardians of the Duat, Celestial Guard or Order of the Jaguar
• Hold shrines for your clan and collect tribute
• League ranks from Wood to Legend — a League season lasts as long as an Alatyr season

PLAY TOGETHER
• Friends, gifts and friendly duels
• Chat, the Catchers’ Order weekly goals and an auction for spirits

EVERY DAY
• Daily quests, wellspring errands, a login streak and seasonal events
• A journal of your adventures and medals for achievements

Available in 13 languages. Play as a guest or sign in with Google, Yandex, VK or Telegram to keep your progress on any device.

«Духолов» — приключение на карте всего мира. Никакого GPS и прогулок под дождём: открой Атлас мира, выбери материк и известный город — Москву, Афины, Рейкьявик, Дублин, Каир, Пекин, Мехико и другие — и шагни во Врата.

ИССЛЕДУЙ ВЕСЬ МИР

• Каждый шаг идёт в зачёт: выводи духов из коконов и поднимайся по Сезонной тропе
